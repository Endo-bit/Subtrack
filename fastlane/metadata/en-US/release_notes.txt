New in this version
- Subscription receipt: share a receipt of all your subscriptions with your yearly total, what you've paid so far and the hours of work it costs.
- Price-hike radar: SubTrack spots when a service raises its price and updates your totals in one tap.
- Rotate, don't stack: see how much you'd save by keeping one streaming service at a time.
- Cheaper or free options for the services you pay for: free plans, family plans and library services.
- Every subscription now shows what it has cost you so far.
- A short monthly summary on the 1st: this month's total and what renews.
- Add your first subscriptions with one tap.
- Choose Light, Dark or Automatic appearance in Settings.
- SubTrack now speaks 20 languages.

Bug fixes
- Reminders now arrive on their own, even if you don't open SubTrack for a long time.
- Billing-day alerts no longer push out the days-before reminder you chose.
- Fixed reminders that could be missing or duplicated after editing subscriptions.
